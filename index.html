Get the KodLokasi app

KodLokasi gives every 10 m x 10 m location in Malaysia a short code. The app shows your own code, searches or scans codes, navigates with Google Maps or Waze, and has a compass. Codes also work in the web app, with no install needed.

Contents

Location data

Names, addresses, service times and GPS coordinates come from the subzone documents supplied by the church (November 2026). Where latitude and longitude were swapped in a document, they have been corrected. Kuching and Miri are positioned from OpenStreetMap (crowd-sourced) because no Sarawak documents were received. Putera Jaya (Telipok) is positioned from its Google Maps listing.

KodLokasi

Each code was obtained from the KodLokasi web app (map.kodlokasi.my) for the coordinates shown on the location page. A KodLokasi code identifies a 10 m x 10 m cell. If a GPS pin is corrected, its code will change.

Photos

Photos were supplied by the church. Locations without a photo show a placeholder.

Disclaimer

This atlas is compiled from church records and open / crowd-sourced data and has not been verified in the field. Positions, addresses and service times may be inaccurate or out of date. It is not for navigation-critical, legal or boundary purposes. Always confirm service times with the local church.
